# Random-Rocket
This website uses NASA's image and video library to display images, videos, and audio clips parterned by their individual descriptions. To find images, videos or audio clips about a specific topic you may use the search bar to do that. Type your interested topic in the search bar, press Enter or the search icon and the page will update.

 You may also generate new individual images, videos or audio clips by clicking their respective "generate" button. If you generate to far you may use the "Previous" button to go back and view your previously generated images, videos, or audio clips.
